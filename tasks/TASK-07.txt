TASK-07: Punctuation & Quote Spacing
- Goal: Implement spacing rules for punctuation and toggle logic for quotes.
- Inputs: Punctuation and Quote tokens.
- Outputs: Buffer with corrected spacing (e.g., `word,` instead of `word ,`).
- Acceptance Criteria:
  1. Punctuation attaches to previous word.
  2. Single quotes toggle `InQuote` state to remove internal spaces.
- Tests:
  - Input: `hello , world` -> `hello, world`
  - Input: `' hello '` -> `'hello'`
